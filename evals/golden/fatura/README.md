# FATURA invoices (golden set)

The `.jpg` images are from **FATURA**, by Mahmoud Limam, Marwa Dhiaf and Yousri Kessentini, published at <https://zenodo.org/records/10371464> under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). They are provided as is, without warranties, as the licence states.

Changes: the images are copied unchanged from `FATURA2.zip` (`invoices_dataset_final/images/`). The `.json` golden records are not FATURA's: they are this project's conversion of FATURA's annotations to the invoice pack's schema, made by [`evals/fatura.py`](../../fatura.py). The recorded model answers about these images are in [`evals/recordings/fatura`](../../recordings/fatura).

Selection, conversion and known limitations: [`docs/DATA.md`](../../../docs/DATA.md#fatura-golden-set-invoice-pack-69).
